Sneaky Pups: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JetSynthesys Private Limited ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and shares information when you play Sneaky Pups: Hidden Object (the "Game", package com.jetsynthesys.findthepuppy) on Android.

The Game has no accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and the Game does not access your precise location, contacts, photos or files.

1. Information we collect

a. Age screen

The Game asks for your birth year when you first play, or you can choose not to say. Your birth year stays on your device. We only send a general age group (such as "13 to 17") with analytics, and we use it to decide whether ads can be shown (see section 5).

b. Gameplay and analytics data

To understand how the Game is played and to improve it, we collect gameplay events through Google Firebase Analytics. These include:

  • Levels started, finished and quit, time spent, and puppies found;
  • Hints and wands used, and in-game items earned and spent;
  • Screens viewed, offers shown, and ads offered and watched;
  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system, app version and general region.

This data is tied to an identifier generated for your installation of the Game, not to your name.

c. Crash and diagnostic data

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix problems. It collects crash reports, the app version, and device and operating system details at the time of the crash.

d. Advertising

The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and the networks it works with: ironSource Ads, Google AdMob and Unity Ads. To show and measure ads, and to personalize them where you allow it, these partners may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location based on your IP address, device information, and how you interact with ads.

Where the law requires it, including the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states, we show a privacy message from Google's User Messaging Platform before ads start. You can decline personalized ads and still play.

e. Purchases

Purchases, including the Puppy Club subscription, are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or billing details. We do receive confirmation of what you bought, and we record the purchase in analytics.

f. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress, settings and privacy choices are stored on your device. We do not offer cloud saves, so uninstalling the Game or clearing its data removes your progress. Reminder notifications, such as the Daily Challenge reminder, are scheduled on your device, and you can turn them off in your device settings.

2. How we use information

  • To run the Game and improve its levels, difficulty and in-game economy;
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs;
  • To change game settings remotely, without an app update;
  • To show and measure ads, and to measure how well our own marketing works;
  • To process and restore purchases.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Personalized ads: make your choice in the privacy message when it appears. You can also delete or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Remove ads: the No Ads purchase and Puppy Club remove interstitial and banner ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.
  • Subscriptions: Puppy Club renews each week until you cancel it in your Google Play account.
  • Stop collection: uninstall the Game to stop further collection.
  • Access and deletion: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to or limit how it is used. These may arise under the EU and UK GDPR, California and other US state laws, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, and Brazil's LGPD. Email dev-jetjapan@jetsynthesys.com and we will respond as the law requires. Because we do not know your name or email, we may need your help to find the data linked to your installation.

How to request deletion of your data

To ask Sneaky Pups: Hidden Object (developer: JetSynthesys) to delete your data:

  • Email dev-jetjapan@jetsynthesys.com with the subject "Sneaky Pups data deletion".
  • Tell us the country you play from and, if you can, your device model and roughly when you played.

What is deleted: analytics and crash data we can link to your installation. To delete your game progress, settings and age answer, uninstall the Game or clear its data, because these are stored only on your device.

What may be kept: records we must keep by law, such as purchase and financial records, and data that we cannot link to you. Google Play keeps its own purchase records, and ad partners keep data under their own policies. You can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.

5. Children

The Game is for a general audience. If the age screen shows a player is under 13, or the player chooses not to give an age, the Game does not start its ad services or show ads, and no advertising ID is collected. Gameplay analytics and crash data may still be collected, without an advertising ID, to keep the Game working.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete what we can find.

6. Data retention

Analytics and crash data are kept for the periods set in our service providers' settings, then deleted. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the Game.

7. Security and international transfers

Data is encrypted in transit. Our service providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States and India. Where required, these transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will also tell players in the Game or on our store listing.
